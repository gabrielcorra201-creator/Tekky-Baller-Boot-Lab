About The Cleat Lab

Built for players who want the perfect cleat — not hype, not marketing, just performance.

Our Mission

The Cleat Lab was created to help players find the right cleat based on fit, feel, and play style — not just brand popularity.

Why We Built This

Most players buy cleats that don’t match their foot shape or how they play. We fix that with a recommendation engine that actually understands soccer boots.
